## 📌 About the Project

KS AI Business Assistant is a Python-based business productivity prototype designed to support startups, entrepreneurs, and small business owners with everyday marketing and business content tasks.

The project provides a simple interactive dashboard where users can enter their business details and generate useful business content.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Simplify everyday business content creation.
- Support startups with marketing ideas.
- Help businesses prepare social media content.
- Provide customer communication templates.
- Encourage better business planning.

## ✨ Features

1. **Business Profile Setup** – Enter business name, industry, and target audience.
2. **Marketing Ideas Generator** – Generate marketing suggestions.
3. **Instagram Caption Generator** – Create social media caption templates.
4. **LinkedIn Post Generator** – Prepare professional LinkedIn post templates.
5. **Customer Reply Generator** – Generate customer response templates.
6. **7-Day Business Action Plan** – Create a basic business activity plan.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- Python
- Google Colab
- IPyWidgets

## ⚙️ How to Run

1. Download the project notebook.
2. Open Google Colab.
3. Upload the `.ipynb` notebook.
4. Run all cells.
5. Enter your business information.
6. Select the required tool.
7. Click Generate Business Output.
